How it works

QR Studio encodes your text, link, or contact details into a scannable QR code entirely inside your browser. Nothing is ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Choose a content type

Pick URL, plain text, email, or Wi-Fi, and fill in the fields. The QR payload is built for you in the right format automatically.

2

Style it

Set foreground and background colors, output size, margin, and error-correction level to balance density against damage tolerance.

3

Preview live

Your browser regenerates the code as you type using an open-source QR encoder — no upload, no waiting on a server.

4

Download

Save your code as a PNG for quick sharing or an SVG for crisp printing at any size. Nothing you typed ever leaves your computer.
