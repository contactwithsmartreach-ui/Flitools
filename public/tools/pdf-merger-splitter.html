How it works

PDF Studio combines and splits documents entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Upload your PDFs

Drag and drop, or click to browse. Merge takes two or more files; Split takes one file you want to break apart.

2

Arrange or choose pages

Reorder files before merging, or specify page ranges — or split into one file per page — before splitting.

3

Process locally

Your browser rebuilds the PDF structure using an open-source library — no upload, no waiting on a server.

4

Download

Download the merged PDF, or the split result as a single file or a .zip. Your originals never leave your computer.
