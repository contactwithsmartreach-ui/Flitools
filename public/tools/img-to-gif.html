How it works

GIF Studio turns a series of still images into one looping animated GIF, entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Upload your images

Drag and drop, or click to browse, 2 or more images. Each image becomes one animation frame, and the order you upload them in is the order they'll play back.

2

Adjust the timing & size

Set the frame delay to control playback speed, choose the output width (height scales automatically), pick whether it loops forever, and balance quality against file size.

3

Generate & preview

Hit "Generate GIF" and your browser encodes every frame locally using an open-source GIF encoder — no upload, no waiting on a server. The finished animation plays instantly in the preview panel.

4

Download

Once it's ready, the Download button saves the finished .gif straight to your device. Your original images and the final file never leave your computer.
