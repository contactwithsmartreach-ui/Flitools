How it works

GIF Studio samples still frames straight out of your video and stitches them into one looping animated GIF, entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Upload your video

Drag and drop, or click to browse, any MP4, WEBM, or MOV file. Your browser reads its duration and dimensions locally — the file itself never leaves your device.

2

Trim & set the frame rate

Pick the start and end time of the clip you want, choose how many frames per second to sample, and set the output width. Fewer frames per second and a smaller width mean a smaller GIF.

3

Generate & preview

Hit "Generate GIF" and your browser seeks through the trimmed range, draws each sampled frame to a canvas, and encodes them into a GIF using an open-source encoder — no server involved. The result plays instantly in the preview panel.

4

Download

Once it's ready, the Download button saves the finished .gif straight to your device. Your original video and the final file never leave your computer.
