How it works

PDF Studio reads, edits, and rebuilds your PDF entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Upload your PDF

Drag and drop or browse for a PDF. Every page is rendered locally into a thumbnail you can click.

2

Rotate pages

Fix a sideways scan one page at a time, or rotate the whole document in a single click.

3

Number & compress

Add page numbers in the position and format you like, and shrink the file size with adjustable compression.

4

Download

Hit "Download PDF" and your finished document is rebuilt and saved straight to your device.
