How it works

Image Studio resizes and re-encodes your photos entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Upload your images

Drag and drop, or click to browse, any number of JPG, PNG, or WebP images. Each one is queued up for processing.

2

Pick format & size

Choose an output format, dial in a quality level, and either scale down by a percentage or cap the maximum width.

3

Process & compare

Your browser redraws each image on a canvas at the new size and re-encodes it — no upload, no waiting on a server. Original vs. new file size is shown for every image.

4

Download

Download images individually or grab them all at once as a .zip. Your originals and the results never leave your computer.
