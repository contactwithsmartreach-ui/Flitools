How it works

Audio Studio decodes, edits, and re-encodes your audio entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Upload your audio

Drag and drop, or click to browse, one or more audio files. Each is decoded locally the moment it's added.

2

Trim & mix

Select a file to preview and trim it, then dial in volume, speed, a 3-band EQ, fades, or reverse.

3

Preview the result

Hit "Preview With Effects" to hear exactly what your export will sound like before you download anything.

4

Export

Download each file separately, or join them all into a single WAV, in the order shown in your file list.
