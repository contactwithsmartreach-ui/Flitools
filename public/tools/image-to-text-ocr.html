How it works

Text Studio reads every letter out of your images and files and turns it into editable text, entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Upload your files

Drag and drop, or click to browse, any number of images or PDFs. Multi-page PDFs are automatically split into one page per scan, in the order you add them.

2

Choose language & enhancement

Pick the language to scan for, toggle contrast enhancement to sharpen faint text, and upscale small images so tiny letters are still readable.

3

Extract & preview

Hit "Extract Text" and your browser reads every page locally using an open-source OCR engine — no upload, no waiting on a server. Every letter lands in the editor.

4

Edit & download

Fix any misreads right in the text box, use find & replace for quick cleanup, then copy or download as .txt or .md. Your files never leave your computer.
