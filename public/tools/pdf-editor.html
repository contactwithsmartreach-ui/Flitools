How it works

PDF Editor operates entirely inside your web browser. No documents are ever uploaded to an external server — everything happens locally on your CPU and GPU.

1

Upload your PDF

Drag and drop any PDF file. Your browser reads each page locally and creates high-res interactive previews.

2

Draw, sign & annotate

Add signatures, text boxes, highlighter strokes, or custom watermarks on any page with customizable colors and line weights.

3

Reorganize pages

Rotate sideways pages or delete unnecessary pages directly from the thumbnail preview list.

4

Export & download

Download your finished document in crisp vector resolution. No files ever leave your computer.
