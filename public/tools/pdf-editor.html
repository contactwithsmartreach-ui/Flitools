How to Edit Text in PDFs Locally

PDF Editor executes everything directly in browser memory without sending your files to any remote server.

1

Upload your PDF

Drag and drop any PDF file. Your browser reads and renders each page in sharp vector resolution.

2

Whiteout & Replace Text

Use the Whiteout tool to cover any existing text you want to change, then click to type your new replacement text over it.

3

Add & Format Text

Click anywhere on the document to add new text blocks. Customize font size, color, bold, and italic styles, and drag to position.

4

Sign & Export

Draw your signature with the pen tool, review pages, and click "Download Edited PDF" to save directly.
