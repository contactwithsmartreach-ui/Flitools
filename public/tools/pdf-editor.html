How it works

PDF Editor reads, modifies, and re-renders text and drawing layers entirely inside your web browser. No files are ever sent to remote servers.

1

Upload your PDF

Drag and drop any PDF file. Your browser parses the font glyphs, layout structure, and pages in real-time.

2

Edit or replace text

Click "Auto-Detect Text" to turn original text into clickable editable blocks, or click anywhere to type additional paragraphs.

3

Whiteout & Sign

Cover unwanted text or logos with the whiteout tool, then draw signatures or highlight key terms with smooth vector pens.

4

Download PDF

Download your finished document in high resolution. Your edited PDF is completely ready to share.
