How it works

Waveform Studio decodes and trims audio entirely inside your browser. Nothing is ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Record or upload

Record straight from your microphone, or drag in an existing MP3, WAV, M4A, or OGG file.

2

See the waveform

Your browser decodes the audio and draws its amplitude over time so you can see exactly where the sound is.

3

Drag to trim

Set a start and end point with the sliders, preview just that selection, and adjust until it's exactly right.

4

Export

Download the trimmed selection as a standard WAV file. Your recording never leaves your computer.
