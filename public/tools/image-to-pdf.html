How it works

PDF Studio turns your photos and scans into a single downloadable PDF, entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything below happens on your own device.

1

Upload your images

Drag and drop, or click to browse, any number of photos or scans. Each one becomes a page, added in the order you drop them.

2

Crop, rotate & reorder

Straighten a sideways scan, crop out anything you don't need, or drag pages into the right order using the arrows on each thumbnail.

3

Choose your layout

Pick a page size, orientation and margin, or let each page fit its image exactly. Everything renders locally, right in your browser.

4

Download your PDF

Hit "Download PDF" and your finished document is assembled and saved straight to your device. Your images never leave your computer.
